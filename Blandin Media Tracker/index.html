Strategic Impact Scoring

A custom scoring model that replaces AVE (Advertising Value Equivalency) — weighted to reflect what actually matters for Blandin Foundation's mission, not generic media circulation figures.

Why This Replaces AVE

Blandin-specific

AVE assigns dollar values based on ad rates and circulation size — which means a passing mention in the Star Tribune looks more valuable than a headline story on KAXE, even though KAXE reaches Blandin's actual mission audience. Strategic Impact Score inverts that logic.

AVE says...
Star Tribune (passing mention)
$8,400 "value"
KAXE Community Radio (headline)
$120 "value"
Based on ad rates & circulation only
→
Strategic Score says...
Star Tribune (passing mention)
Score: 32
KAXE Community Radio (headline)
Score: 94
Weighted for Blandin's actual mission reach
